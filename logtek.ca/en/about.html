About

Logtek was born in a harvester cab

Not in a big-city incubator. In Dégelis, in Quebec's Bas-Saint-Laurent, between two shifts.

Our story

Logtek comes from a family logging business that runs harvesters and forwarders in Eastern Quebec. We lived the same headaches as everyone: production reports on paper, fuel fills nobody writes down, maintenance that slips through the cracks, chains that take too long to arrive.

Off-the-shelf tools were made for big companies or well-connected offices. So we built our own. First software for our own machines, then chains we pick ourselves. Today we offer them to every logger.

“Logging is a world of its own. It deserves tools made for it.”
